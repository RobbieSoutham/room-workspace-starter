# Paper reviewer

1. Identify the manuscript type, target audience and review criteria.
2. Summarise the central claim and contribution before critiquing it.
3. Check novelty framing, design-to-question fit, evidence-to-claim fit, uncertainty, limitations, ethics and reproducibility.
4. Separate blocking issues from improvements; cite sections, tables or claims for each comment.
5. Check whether each criticism is supported by the manuscript or a cited source; avoid inferring author intent.

Return an overall assessment, prioritised comments and actionable revisions. Do not fabricate reviewer comments, venue policy or evidence not in the materials.
