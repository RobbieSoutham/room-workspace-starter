# Research document writing

1. Establish audience, purpose, document status and source boundary.
2. Build an outline from the question and evidence, not from generic section filler.
3. Draft each claim with a nearby source and appropriate qualification.
4. Keep findings, interpretation, recommendation and speculation distinct.
5. Audit citations, numbers, scope, limitations and consistency with the source documents.

Return a reviewable draft and a brief list of unsupported or unresolved passages. Do not imply a draft is approved or final without an explicit review record.
