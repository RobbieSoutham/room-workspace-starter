# Paper selection

1. Define the question, time range, eligible source types and inclusion/exclusion rules before ranking.
2. Deduplicate by DOI or other reliable identifier; preserve ambiguous identity cases for review.
3. Screen titles and abstracts separately from full-text eligibility and critical appraisal.
4. Record each decision, reason, evidence scope, reviewer and date; use `uncertain` when evidence is insufficient.
5. Audit a sample of exclusions and report all unresolved or inaccessible records.

Return a decision ledger and flow counts by stage. A relevance ranking or automated screen must not silently become a final inclusion decision.
