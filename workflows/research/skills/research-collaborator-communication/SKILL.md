# Research collaborator communication

Use for drafting or reviewing a message; sending requires the appropriate local authorisation and channel procedure.

1. Identify the purpose, recipient role and requested action.
2. Use only supplied and verified project facts; distinguish decisions from proposals.
3. Put the key context and question first; keep sensitive details to the minimum needed.
4. Check names, dates, attachments, claims and tone against the source material.
5. Return a draft and flag any missing fact that could change its meaning.

Do not send messages, invent commitments or disclose unpublished work beyond the intended recipients.
