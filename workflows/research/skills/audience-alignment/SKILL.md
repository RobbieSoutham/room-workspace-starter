# Audience alignment

Use before drafting or revising a talk, poster, paper summary or stakeholder update.

1. Name the audience by role and task, not by stereotype. Record what they know, need to decide and are likely to question.
2. Ground these assumptions in supplied evidence, prior feedback or a clearly labelled hypothesis. Do not invent individual preferences.
3. Map each audience need to the output's claim, evidence, explanation and next step.
4. Check terminology, context, accessibility, density and the cost of a misunderstanding.
5. Revise the output, then list what remains uncertain and what feedback would resolve it.

Return a short audience profile and a fit audit tied to the artifact. Do not let audience appeal change the evidence or hide limitations.
