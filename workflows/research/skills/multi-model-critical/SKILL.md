# Multi-model critical review

Use when the consequence of an unnoticed error justifies the extra cost. Do not use model count as a proxy for correctness.

1. Freeze the claim, sources, question and known constraints.
2. Give independent reviewers the same evidence and ask each to check specific risks: citation support, counterevidence, method and scope.
3. Keep their first-pass reviews blind to one another and to the preferred conclusion where practical.
4. Compare disagreements against primary sources. Resolve only with evidence; otherwise retain uncertainty or abstain.
5. Record model/version, prompt, source boundary, material disagreements and human adjudication.

Return a short decision table and unresolved issues. Agreement among models is not independent empirical verification; a human remains responsible for claim-bearing decisions.
