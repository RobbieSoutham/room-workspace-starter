# Model access review

Use for selecting or renewing model access, or tracking a potentially useful offer. Follow the canonical [model access and offers process](../../../../docs/model-access-and-offers.md) and the [capacity and cost review](../capacity-and-cost-review/SKILL.md) where relevant.

1. Define the workload, data/privacy needs, users and decision horizon.
2. Check the provider's official offer and product terms; record the checked date and eligibility. Mark third-party claims `UNVERIFIED`.
3. Distinguish chat/coding subscription, metered API, promotional credits, institutional access and open-weight deployment.
4. Compare usable value, cost after expiry, caps, data terms, reliability, portability and operational burden against the current/no-change option.
5. Identify the payment owner, usage cap, trial stop date and post-credit action before activation.
6. Keep personal account identifiers, balances, credentials and verification documents out of shared files and version control.
7. Report the source links, checked date, assumptions, expiry/recheck date and recommendation. Do not apply, purchase or change billing without explicit authorization.
