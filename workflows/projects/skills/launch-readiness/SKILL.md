# Launch readiness

Use before releasing a product, service, report or other deliverable to its intended audience.

1. Define the release, audience, owner, channel, date and explicit scope. Confirm who can authorise release.
2. Check acceptance criteria, known defects, rollback or correction path, monitoring and support ownership.
3. Review privacy, security, rights/licensing, accessibility, legal or regulatory obligations relevant to this release; record specialist reviews still needed.
4. Confirm documentation, user communication, dependencies and recovery steps are ready and consistent with the released version.
5. Record each check as pass, fail, not applicable or unknown with evidence. Escalate blockers; do not release on the basis of an unchecked checklist or treat this review as authorisation.
