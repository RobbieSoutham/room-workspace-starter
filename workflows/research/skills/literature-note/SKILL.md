# Literature note

1. Verify bibliographic identity from the paper or authoritative metadata.
2. Summarise the research question, design, data, findings and authors' stated limitations.
3. Add exact page or section locators for quotations and claim-bearing details.
4. Separate the authors' claims from your interpretation and relevance to the current question.
5. Link related notes by topic or method; mark unanswered fields rather than guessing.

Return one note with stable identifiers, access scope and a short relevance statement. Do not infer methods or results from a title or abstract when full text is needed.
