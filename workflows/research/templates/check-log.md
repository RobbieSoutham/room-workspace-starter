# Check log

Append a new row for each artifact version. Keep earlier failures and blocked checks visible.

| Date | Artifact/version | Check and criterion | Status | Evidence/source | Limitation | Action / next check |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| YYYY-MM-DD | `<path, ID or hash>` | `<what was checked>` | `NOT_RUN` | `<evidence>` | `<known limit>` | `<next action>` |

Allowed statuses: `PASS`, `PASS_WITH_LIMITATIONS`, `FAIL`, `BLOCKED`, `NOT_RUN`, `NOT_APPLICABLE`.
