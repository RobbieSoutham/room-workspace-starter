# Research frame

Use this skill when a project is starting, its research question has changed, or a planned method is at risk of driving the question. Do not rerun the framing exercise for routine edits to work that already has a current frame.

## Workflow

1. Read the canonical [research principles](../../knowledge/research-principles.md) and check the project's existing frame before proposing a new one.
2. Clarify the research question, its purpose and scope, plausible alternatives, and what evidence would support, weaken or leave the claim unresolved.
3. Mark the work exploratory, confirmatory or another appropriate mode. Do not present exploration as if it tested a pre-specified claim.
4. Sketch the method and the specific artefact a reader will inspect. Check that the method can produce the evidence the question needs.
5. Identify the main validity threats, ethics/data boundaries and reproducibility details that need a protocol.
6. If asked to record the frame, copy and complete [`../../templates/research_frame.md`](../../templates/research_frame.md) in the project repository. Preserve prior versions and log material revisions.

If an answer is unknown, record it as an open issue rather than filling it with an assumption. A frame structures the question; it does not establish that the method or claim is valid.
