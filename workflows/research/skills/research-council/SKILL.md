# Research council

Use when a question benefits from genuinely different analytical roles.

1. Freeze the question, evidence boundary and desired decision.
2. Have a builder propose the strongest defensible answer, a critic identify unsupported links, and a red team seek counterevidence and alternative explanations.
3. Keep first-pass analyses independent and require citations to primary sources for factual claims.
4. Synthesize disagreement by evidence, not vote. Mark unresolved issues and abstain where evidence is insufficient.
5. Record sources searched, roles, material changes and a verification plan.

Return a concise memo and evidence ledger. More reviewers do not compensate for shared sources, shared blind spots or weak methods.
