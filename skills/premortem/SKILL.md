# Premortem

Use before changes with meaningful financial, operational, privacy or delivery consequences. For a small reversible action, keep the review brief or skip it.

1. State the plan and imagine it has failed by a named date.
2. List plausible causes as causal chains, not generic risks.
3. For each, name the hidden assumption, early warning sign and mitigation or stop condition.
4. Rank likelihood and consequence separately.
5. Revise the plan and identify tripwires that trigger a pause or rollback.

Keep the review proportional. A premortem informs a decision; it does not itself authorise the action.
