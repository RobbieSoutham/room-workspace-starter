# Evidence and claims

**Owner:** Research room · **Status:** Canonical source of truth for evidence traceability, citation handling and claim review.

## Evidence record

Keep a durable route from each important claim to the evidence used to support, qualify or challenge it. Copy [`../templates/evidence_ledger.csv`](../templates/evidence_ledger.csv) into the project repository and adapt fields to the method. Each row should identify:

- the claim or question it relates to;
- the source or observation and a stable locator;
- the relevant passage, result or observation, with quotation distinguished from paraphrase;
- how it bears on the claim;
- uncertainty, source limitations and review state.

Keep source findings separate from the researcher's interpretation. Preserve contradictory, null and negative evidence. Store restricted source material only in its approved location; use the ledger to point to it without copying sensitive content into this public template.

## Literature-search record

When a review needs to be reproducible, record its question and scope, databases or archives, query strings, search date, filters, eligibility criteria, deduplication method, screening decisions and synthesis approach. Give reasons for material exclusions. Do not describe a search as exhaustive or systematic unless the method supports that description.

Adapt this record to the review type and field. Follow any required review protocol and reporting standard.

## Claim review

Review each material statement in a paper, report, presentation or decision against the source itself and its context. Check exact identity, passage, qualifiers, population or setting, method, quantities and the strength of the inference. A citation's presence does not prove that it supports the nearby claim; an exact quote match does not establish that the overall interpretation is valid.

Use these labels consistently in a project audit:

| Label | Meaning |
|---|---|
| `SUPPORTED` | The inspected evidence directly supports the claim as written. |
| `PARTIALLY_SUPPORTED` | The evidence supports only part of the claim or a narrower version. |
| `NOT_SUPPORTED` | The inspected evidence does not support the claim. |
| `CONTRADICTED` | The inspected evidence materially conflicts with the claim. |
| `UNTRACEABLE` | The cited source, passage or calculation could not be located or checked. |

For numerical statements, link the source data and calculation or output needed to reproduce the value. Narrow or remove claims with partial support; do not silently retain a stronger headline.

## Reporting and update rule

Keep citations and provenance beside claim-bearing notes. Check cited material against primary sources where possible, and verify bibliographic details before circulation. A generated summary or citation suggestion is a search lead, not evidence. Update this file when shared claim-review practice changes; record project-specific verdicts in the project audit or evidence ledger.
