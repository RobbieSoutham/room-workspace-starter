# Research knowledge

This directory is the research room's canonical home for reusable principles and practice. Update these documents when the room's research standards change.

| Source-of-truth document | Owns |
|---|---|
| [`research-principles.md`](research-principles.md) | Framing questions, understanding prior evidence, method fit and claim scope |
| [`evidence-and-claims.md`](evidence-and-claims.md) | Evidence provenance, literature-search records, citations and claim-to-evidence links |
| [`reproducibility-and-integrity.md`](reproducibility-and-integrity.md) | Protocols, deviations, negative findings, ethics, review and responsible release |
| [`communication-and-visual-design.md`](communication-and-visual-design.md) | Audience-led research communication, evidence boundaries and truthful visual design |

Room-local skills in `../skills/` apply these principles to recurring tasks. Templates in `../templates/` are copied into individual project repositories and completed there. Project evidence, data and decisions belong in their approved project source of truth, not in this generic starter.

Add durable room-level methods or decisions here only when they are reusable across projects. Preserve citations and provenance for claim-bearing knowledge.
