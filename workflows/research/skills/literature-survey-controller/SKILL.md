# Literature survey controller

1. Define the survey question, date boundary, source types and intended coverage; do not imply exhaustiveness without a method that supports it.
2. Split the question into distinct search strands and assign a query, source and stop rule to each.
3. Maintain one candidate ledger with identifiers, provenance, deduplication and screening status.
4. Sample for missed synonyms or disciplines, revise the query with a dated rationale, and preserve earlier versions.
5. Build evidence cards for included sources, then synthesize themes, contradictions and gaps.
6. Run a citation audit and report search dates, exclusions, inaccessible sources and residual coverage limits.

Return the survey report plus search log, candidate ledger and evidence map. Do not mix search volume with strength of evidence.
