# Manuscript review

Use for a developmental or pre-submission review. For source-by-source support checking, use `claim-evidence-audit`.

1. Confirm the manuscript version, intended audience or venue, review scope and any supplied criteria.
2. Summarise the central question, contribution and argument as you understand them; flag where the text does not make these clear.
3. Review alignment among question, evidence, method, results and conclusions. Check that limitations, uncertainty and alternative explanations receive appropriate treatment.
4. Assess structure, definitions, transitions, tables/figures and citation placement at the requested level.
5. Give prioritised, actionable comments with locations and explain the consequence of each issue. Separate essential corrections from optional polish; do not invent reviewer or venue requirements.
