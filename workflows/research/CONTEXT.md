# Research room

## Purpose

Manage research questions, evidence, methods and academic outputs.

## Boundaries

- Keep claims tied to their evidence and note uncertainty.
- Keep project-specific research data in approved storage, not in this starter repository.

## Canonical files

- Active research priorities: `CURRENT.md`
- Research principles: `knowledge/research-principles.md`
- Evidence and claim handling: `knowledge/evidence-and-claims.md`
- Reproducibility and integrity: `knowledge/reproducibility-and-integrity.md`
- Project records: copy templates from `templates/` into the project repository
- Fictional example: `examples/search-strategy-evaluation.md`
- Durable research knowledge: `knowledge/`
- Shared workspace rules: repository `CLAUDE.md` and `SOURCE_OF_TRUTH.md`

## Room skills

- `research-frame` — define or revise a question and its research frame
- `evidence-led-review` — plan and synthesise a literature review with a traceable search and evidence record
- `research-protocol` — specify a method, analysis plan, reproducibility details and deviations before claim-bearing work
- `claim-evidence-audit` — check whether citations and evidence support the wording and scope of claims

Skills implement the canonical documents above; they do not establish separate research policy.
