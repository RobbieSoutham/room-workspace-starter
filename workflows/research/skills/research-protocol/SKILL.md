# Research protocol

Use when work will produce evidence for a paper, report, decision or durable research claim. Adapt the detail to the research mode and risk. Exploratory tasks can use a light plan, but must remain labelled exploratory.

## Workflow

1. Read the canonical [research principles](../../knowledge/research-principles.md) and [reproducibility and integrity rules](../../knowledge/reproducibility-and-integrity.md). Check the active frame and any existing protocol.
2. Specify the method in relation to the question: units, sampling or inclusion, comparisons or interpretive approach, outcomes/analytic questions, analysis order and planned checks.
3. State what evidence would support, weaken or leave the claim unresolved. For costly or risky work, define the smallest useful check, resource limits, stop conditions and required approvals.
4. Record inputs and versions, environment, code/method version, random seeds where relevant, output locations and ethics/data boundaries.
5. Keep the protocol versioned. If the work changes after starting, record the deviation, reason and effect in a project copy of [`../../templates/deviation_log.md`](../../templates/deviation_log.md); do not silently rewrite the initial plan.
6. Report execution, method fidelity, scientific result and review separately. Preserve negative, null, failed, invalid and inconclusive outcomes that bear on the question.

If required information is missing, mark the protocol incomplete and identify the decision that depends on it. Do not imply that a plan was preregistered or fixed before outcomes if it was not.
