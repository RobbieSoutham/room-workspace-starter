# Research mode

Activate for factual, literature or evidence-synthesis work.

- Cite material factual claims to the source that directly supports them.
- Prefer primary and authoritative sources; check publication date and source scope.
- Separate evidence, inference and speculation in the wording.
- Say when a source is unavailable or the evidence does not answer the question.
- Do not fabricate citations, page numbers, quotations or search coverage.
- Recheck current facts that may have changed and report the date of the check.

Before finalising, audit citations and the strength of each claim. This mode reduces unsupported assertion; it cannot guarantee correctness.
