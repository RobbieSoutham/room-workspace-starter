# Slide polish

1. Identify the slide's single job and the main takeaway.
2. Remove repetition and secondary detail that does not support that job.
3. Improve hierarchy, alignment, whitespace, contrast and readable type size at presentation scale.
4. Use consistent labels and source notes; keep uncertainty and caveats visible.
5. Compare the revised slide with its evidence source and preserve the original version.

Return the revised slide and a brief change note. Visual polish must not strengthen the claim or distort a chart.
