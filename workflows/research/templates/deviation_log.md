# Deviation and decision log

Copy this file into the project repository. Preserve the original protocol or plan; add dated records here instead of silently rewriting it after work begins.

| Date | Planned choice | What changed | Reason and evidence available at the time | Effect on analysis or interpretation | Approval or review |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|

## Unplanned observations and negative findings

Record relevant outcomes that do not support the preferred explanation, including failed controls, null results, invalid cases, non-estimable outcomes and unexpected observations. Link to the source record without copying restricted data into this log.

## Decision record

For decisions that materially affect the claim, record:

- the options considered;
- evidence available before deciding;
- the choice and reason;
- who reviewed or approved it;
- what observation would prompt reconsideration.
