# Citation audit

Use before sharing, submission or a substantial revision.

1. Extract every in-text citation and map it to one reference record.
2. Resolve author, title, year, venue and persistent identifier against the source or an authoritative catalogue.
3. Find unmatched references, uncited entries, duplicate identities and malformed metadata.
4. For claim-bearing citations, inspect the cited source and record whether it supports the exact claim and scope.
5. Preserve missing-source and inaccessible-source cases as explicit limitations; do not fill fields by guessing.

Return a discrepancy table with manuscript locator, source record, issue and action. Citation presence alone does not establish evidential support.
