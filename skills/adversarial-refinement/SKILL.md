# Adversarial refinement

Use when a proposal is consequential, uncertain or likely to benefit from deliberate critique. Do not add a debate ritual to routine work.

1. State the proposal, intended outcome, constraints and decision owner.
2. Build the strongest practical case for it, then identify assumptions that must hold.
3. Develop the strongest counterargument and realistic alternatives.
4. Compare options against the stated outcome and constraints; separate evidence from judgement.
5. Revise the proposal or state what evidence or decision would resolve the disagreement.

Keep criticism specific and useful. Do not manufacture balance when evidence strongly favours one option, and do not present speculation as a finding.
