# Meeting note distillation

1. Preserve the date, meeting type and participants only where appropriate to share.
2. Separate decisions, discussion, evidence cited, actions, owners and deadlines.
3. Mark unclear attribution or ambiguous wording for confirmation; do not invent consensus.
4. Link actions to the relevant project or question and carry unresolved items forward.
5. Keep confidential or personal material out of public project notes.

Return concise minutes and an action list. Distillation should preserve decision context, not turn tentative discussion into an approved result.
