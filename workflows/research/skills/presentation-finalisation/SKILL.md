# Presentation finalisation

1. Confirm the deck version, talk length, audience and source manuscript.
2. Check each cited claim against its source and ensure references resolve.
3. Review narrative order, transitions, density, timing and the takeaway.
4. Inspect rendered slides for clipping, legibility, contrast, figure labels and speaker-note consistency.
5. Record unresolved issues and the exact version reviewed.

Return a prioritised checklist with slide numbers and pass/fail state. Do not silently edit a frozen final deck; make a new version and preserve the review history.
