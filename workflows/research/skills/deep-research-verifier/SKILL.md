# Deep research verifier

Use for difficult questions where a wrong synthesis would materially mislead.

1. Turn the question into answerable subquestions and identify what would count against the leading answer.
2. Search a bounded set of primary and authoritative sources; log queries, dates and inclusion decisions.
3. Keep an evidence ledger linking each claim to exact source passages, source type and limitations.
4. Compare contradictory sources and distinguish direct evidence, inference and speculation.
5. Ask an independent verifier to inspect critical claims and citations without inheriting the draft's conclusion.
6. Stop when the planned source boundary is met; disclose remaining gaps.

Return a decision-ready synthesis, evidence ledger and verification status. More search volume is not a substitute for source quality or a stopping rule.
