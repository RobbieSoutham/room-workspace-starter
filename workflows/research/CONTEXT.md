# Research room

## Purpose

Manage research questions, evidence, methods and academic outputs.

## Boundaries

- Keep claims tied to their evidence and note uncertainty.
- Keep project-specific research data in approved storage, not in this starter repository.

## Canonical files

- Active research priorities: `CURRENT.md`
- Reusable research principles and evidence practice: `knowledge/`
- Research workflow and source-based guidance: repository `docs/`
- Skill map: `docs/research-skill-map.md`
- Evidence and failed-check record: `docs/evidence-and-failure-records.md` and `templates/check-log.md`
- Project records: copy templates from `templates/` into the project repository
- Fictional examples: `examples/`
- Durable research knowledge: `knowledge/`
- Shared workspace rules: repository `CLAUDE.md` and `SOURCE_OF_TRUTH.md`

## Room skills

- `audience-alignment` — Define an evidence-based audience profile and check whether a research output fits its readers.
- `brainstorm` — Develop a research idea into a testable, source-aware set of options.
- `citation-audit` — Check citation identity, completeness and claim support in research writing.
- `claim-evidence-audit` — Check whether citations, data and calculations support claims in research writing, reports or presentations.
- `connected-paper-search` — Expand a literature search from several seed papers using citation links and related-work services.
- `corpus-rag-synthesis` — Answer a research question from a bounded, user-supplied paper corpus with claim-level citations.
- `deck-build` — Turn research evidence into a structured presentation draft and review its narrative.
- `deep-research-verifier` — Run a bounded, source-grounded investigation with an evidence ledger and independent verification.
- `evidence-finder` — Locate exact passages and assess whether a source supports a research claim.
- `evidence-led-review` — Plan, conduct or synthesise a literature review with a traceable search and evidence trail.
- `experiment-lifecycle` — Specify, implement and close out a claim-bearing computational experiment with evidence gates.
- `figure-generation` — Select and build a research figure that accurately communicates its data or method.
- `hypothesis-generation` — Generate and prioritise falsifiable research hypotheses grounded in a defined literature base.
- `knowledge-base-sync` — Maintain a research knowledge base without creating competing copies or overwriting local work.
- `latex-authoring` — Draft and revise LaTeX research documents while preserving citations, structure and reproducibility.
- `literature-note` — Create a structured, source-linked note for one research paper.
- `literature-note-intake` — Triage a newly found paper into a verified bibliographic record and literature note.
- `literature-survey-controller` — Coordinate a broad literature survey with bounded search strands, a candidate ledger and evidence-based synthesis.
- `literature-synthesis` — Synthesize a defined set of sources into a traceable account of themes, disagreements and gaps.
- `model-evaluation` — Evaluate candidate models and research agents on representative, task-specific work using reproducible conditions and human-checked outcomes.
- `manuscript-review` — Review a research manuscript for argument, structure, methods reporting and claim calibration.
- `meeting-note-distill` — Turn research meeting notes into an accurate record of decisions, evidence, actions and open questions.
- `multi-model-critical` — Use independent model reviews to find errors in high-consequence research synthesis, with evidence-based adjudication.
- `paper-code-discovery` — Investigate whether a paper's software or data are publicly available and usable.
- `paper-reviewer` — Review a research manuscript for contribution, evidence, methods, reporting and clarity.
- `paper-scaffold` — Turn a defined research question or result into a source-aware manuscript structure.
- `paper-selection` — Select candidate papers using explicit relevance and evidence-access criteria.
- `paper-summary` — Create a concise, evidence-linked summary of a research paper for later synthesis.
- `poster-build` — Design a scientific poster that communicates a bounded research result clearly and accurately.
- `presentation-finalisation` — Run a final source, narrative and rendering audit on a research presentation.
- `research-collaborator-communication` — Draft accurate research correspondence for a collaborator, editor or research group.
- `research-communication` — Adapt established research for a named audience, format and purpose without changing its evidential strength.
- `research-council` — Structure independent builder, critic and red-team review of a research question or evidence synthesis.
- `research-document-writing` — Draft or revise research plans, summaries and synthesis documents from supplied evidence.
- `research-frame` — Frame or revise a research question before substantial evidence collection, analysis or implementation.
- `research-knowledge-context` — Load a bounded research knowledge base to provide relevant context without treating notes as primary evidence.
- `research-mode` — Enforce source-grounded answers, citations and explicit uncertainty during research tasks.
- `research-protocol` — Specify a research method and reproducibility record before claim-bearing data collection, analysis or computational work.
- `research-question-development` — Turn a topic or problem into a focused, answerable research question and explicit scope.
- `research-task-dashboard` — Maintain a concise view of active research work, decisions and next actions.
- `self-improving-workflow` — Improve a reusable research procedure through versioned changes, empirical checks and reversible rollout.
- `slide-polish` — Improve slide clarity, visual hierarchy and audience fit without changing the research evidence.
- `venue-alignment` — Compare candidate journals or conferences against a manuscript's contribution, audience and verified requirements.

Skills implement the canonical documents above; they do not establish separate research policy.
