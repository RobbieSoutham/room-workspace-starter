# Venue alignment

1. Summarise the manuscript's contribution, methods, evidence maturity and intended readership.
2. Identify candidate venues from relevant published work and the manuscript's field.
3. Check each candidate's current official aims, scope, article types, review model, policies, fees and deadlines.
4. Compare topic and contribution fit with recent papers; distinguish evidence from reputation or ranking proxies.
5. Flag feasibility, open-access, rights and administrative constraints; never infer acceptance likelihood from a metric alone.

Return a ranked comparison with source links, checked date and uncertainties. Verify rules from official venue pages immediately before submission.
