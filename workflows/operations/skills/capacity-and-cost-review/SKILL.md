# Capacity and cost review

Use before sizing, scaling, migrating or committing to recurring infrastructure spend.

1. State the workload, service objective, planning horizon and decision being made.
2. Gather current usage, peaks, growth assumptions, limits, headroom and major cost drivers from dated evidence.
3. Compare viable options on capacity, reliability, total cost, operational burden, lock-in and reversibility. Mark estimates and assumptions.
4. Include a low-cost or no-change baseline and a stop condition for trials where useful. Do not extrapolate a short sample as stable demand without qualification.
5. Recommend the smallest option that meets the stated need, explain uncertainty, and name what must be measured before commitment. Do not purchase or change billing without explicit authority.
